Tic-tac-toe with a blocking CPU, built by Qwen2.5-Coder-14B-Instruct (9GB, 4-bit, on a laptop). The whole engine — win detection, board status, legal moves and the CPU's reply — is the model's own code, written one function at a time against a fixed contract and graded mechanically: 4/4 parts at pass@3, blocking all 24 threat shapes. The grid, rendering and reset are harness code. Back to its leaderboard card ↗

Part of the INCHWORM app-builder gallery: models scored on TinyMark are asked to build small apps. Asking for one pure function at a time against a fixed host is what made this one land — the same model never produced a correct blocking CPU in fifteen whole-app attempts. Nothing here is hand-edited: every function is model output, and the score comes from a Node gate, not an opinion.
